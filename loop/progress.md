# Loop progress

The project began on 2026-10-30 with a feasibility study of Open5GS and Bend 2.

## Summary (2026-11-16)

- **Milestone 1: tickets 01–23 done**, and the hardening tickets 32–38 the owner's review of 2026-11-03 asked for.
- **Milestone 2, handover within one AMF and one UPF: tickets 24–31 done.** 25 of the 42 advisories are in scope, each ruled out by its laws (`loop/acceptance-report.md`); the flake checks `e2e` (registration, Xn and N2 handover) and `edge-sctp` pass.
- **Milestone 2 review fixes (2026-11-07):** one Path Switch walk (`Pw`) and one N2 handover walk (`Hw`) in `kernel/proofs/walk.bend` serve every proof; each handover message finds its UE through one lookup (`Ho.by_owner`, `Ho.by_target`, `N.Ue.keyed`); both handovers repoint sessions through `kernel/smf/switch.bend`; a Handover Required to a target released before it answered is refused (`amf.handover.responses_correlated`); the e2e checks eUPF's downlink FAR after each handover. Not done, still open: `amf.ncc.step` (every step stores an unchanged, next or initial {NH, NCC}) and the `amf.handover.guarded` clause that no step stops a pending handover's guard. Each is a property of every procedure's outcome, so it needs evidence at every leaf of the liveness walk, or a kernel where `N.Step.of` derives UE guard stops as it derives session ones (`N.Gone.cancels`).
- **Tickets 39, 41 and 42, the Session Deletion rework:** a Session Deletion Request leaves its session `Deleting`, holding its IPv4, TEID and SEID, and goes again up to N1 = 3 times (TS 29.244 §6.4); whatever starts deleting a session sends the request and starts its guard for T1 (`smf.deletion.armed`), nothing but that guard or the matching Session Deletion Response touches the row or its guard (`smf.deletion.held`), and either ends it (`smf.deletion.retransmitted`, `smf.deletion.answered`), so the row is freed within (N1 + 1) × T1. GHSA-855g-699w-9w28 is proven again: all 25 in-scope advisories are. Also from ticket 39: NG Setup before any UE message (`amf.ng_setup.first`), contexts owned by set-up associations (`amf.contexts.set_up`), one route lemma, scripts in nushell and session hooks in Python.
- **The ticket 39 retro's fixes 1–3:** `acceptance.py --base origin/main` fails on a law restated since `main` that `loop/law-changes.md` doesn't list; `scripts/check.nu` runs `--verdict` alone and the kernel tests once (about 5 min, was about 20); `scripts/test-kernel.nu` takes test names and runs 6 at a time.
- **Next:** ticket 40 items 1–3 (NG Setup ending handovers, `amf.ncc.step` and `amf.handover.guarded`, the N2 and N1 session releases); items 4–6 are done.
