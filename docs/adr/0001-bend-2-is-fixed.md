# Bend 2 is the kernel's language, with no fallback

In late 2026, Bend 2 had an unaudited compiler, no 64-bit integers, no SCTP, no IPv6 and frequent breaking changes. Lean 4, F* and Rocq were mature alternatives. We chose Bend 2 anyway and keep no fallback language, betting that its team closes those gaps. We report the gaps upstream rather than switch languages.
